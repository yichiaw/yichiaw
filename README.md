# Hi, I'm Yi-Chia Wang 👋

**Research Faculty** · Principal Research Scientist at [Carnegie Mellon University](https://hcii.cmu.edu/), School of Computer Science, Human-Computer Interaction Institute (HCII)

I study **human–human and human–AI interaction** in social contexts, and how those interactions shape engagement, well-being, integrity, and fairness. My work sits at the intersection of **Computational Social Science**, **Natural Language Processing**, and **AI**, combining conversation modeling with large-scale data analysis, field experiments, and real-world deployment.

### What I'm working on

- **LLM-based training for customer-service professionals** — simulation-based conversational training with realistic virtual customers and personalized feedback, built with industry partners (e.g., Teleperformance), to develop skills such as stress management and de-escalation.
- **Scalable mental-health support training** — LLM-based agents for experiential micro-skills training (empathy, active listening, feedback) on large online support platforms (e.g., 7 Cups).
- **Trustworthy and socially aware LLMs** — evaluation frameworks, moral and social reasoning benchmarks, and safety guardrails for large language models.
